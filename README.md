## Daniel Davis

**Bayesian inference and machine learning for ill-posed inverse problems on physical sensor data.**

I solve ill-posed inverse problems on large, noisy physical sensor data with Bayesian inference, machine learning, and HPC. Before my PhD I spent 8+ years in industrial R&D at PETRONAS, where that work produced measurable results: ~80% less training data for a hybrid neural network and an estimated ~USD 200k/day saved through simulation-driven survey design.

### Currently

PhD candidate in Geophysics at the Colorado School of Mines, Center for Wave Phenomena (CWP). Expected 2028.
Open to **AI research scientist / research engineer** roles and **summer 2027 PhD research internships**.

Now working on multimodal trans-dimensional Bayesian inversion of surface-wave dispersion, scaled to 3-D on HPC, and planning a benchmark of LLM reasoning on geophysical inverse problems.

### Focus areas

- **Bayesian inference with unknown model complexity:** reversible-jump MCMC that infers the number of model parameters along with their values, with full uncertainty.
- **Signal from noise:** extracting coherent sub-2 Hz wavefields from long ambient recordings via cross-correlation (seismic interferometry).
- **PDE-constrained inversion:** building starting models for inverting wave simulations against data (full waveform inversion).
- **ML for physical fields:** hybrid CNN + RNN models that learn subsurface property fields from wave data.
